Helpful hint
Say only your first name.
That is all the check asks for. Say it clearly, in your normal testing voice:
“______”
  • Wear the headset with the microphone — the small mic on the arm, not the Chromebook's built-in one.
  • Position the mic near the corner of your mouth, about a thumb's width away — not right in front of your lips.
  • Normal talking voice — not a whisper, not a shout.
  • In the playback, listen for the ending sound of your name. If it is fuzzy, adjust the mic and record again.
  • Hear buzzing or nothing at all? Raise your hand before the test starts, not during it.
